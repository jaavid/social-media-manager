# Code of Conduct

Participation must be respectful and free of harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity/expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity/orientation.
Respect differing views, accept constructive feedback, focus on community benefit and show empathy. Harassment, insults, trolling, disruptive behavior and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainer [@jaavid](https://github.com/jaavid). Maintainers review complaints fairly and protect the reporter's privacy. They may remove/reject contributions or temporarily/permanently ban contributors for violations.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
